# Lectures

Slide decks, recorded talks and conference presentations about GLACIER and the
ideas behind it.

## Available now

No slide deck or recording has been published on this site yet.

The public record of the work presented so far is in the conference papers
listed below. They are the closest thing to a lecture currently available, and
they are the right thing to cite.

## Conference papers

### Frost: A Simulation Platform for Early Validation and Testing of Manufacturing Software

Pietro Turco, Sebastiano Gaiardelli, Enrico Fraccaroli, Michele Lora,
Samarjit Chakraborty, Franco Fummi.
*2025 IEEE 23rd International Conference on Industrial Informatics (INDIN)*,
12–15 July 2025. IEEE.

The paper introducing Frost. Cited by the
[frost repository](https://github.com/glacier-project/frost) as its preferred
citation.

### Distributed Control Logic Validation through Lingua Franca

Pietro Turco, Sebastiano Gaiardelli, Enrico Fraccaroli, Dong Seon Cheng,
Michele Lora, Franco Fummi.
*2026 IEEE 24th International Conference on Industrial Informatics (INDIN)*.
IEEE.

### xPPU-Frost: A Testbed for Evaluating Deterministic Industrial Automation Strategies

Samuele Santacà, Sebastiano Gaiardelli, Franco Fummi.
*2026 IEEE 24th International Conference on Industrial Informatics (INDIN)*,
26–29 July 2026. IEEE.

The preferred citation for
[xppu-frost](https://github.com/glacier-project/xppu-frost).

!!! note "Citing the software"

    Both `frost` and `xppu-frost` carry a `CITATION.cff` file, so GitHub's
    "Cite this repository" button produces a correct BibTeX or APA entry
    directly. Prefer that to transcribing the details above.

## What a published lecture will look like

An entry will carry:

- the title and who gave it;
- when and where it was given;
- the audience it assumes;
- a link to the slides or the recording.
