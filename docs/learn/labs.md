# Labs and exercises

Hands-on sessions and exercise sets, usually run alongside a taught course.

## Available now

No lab or exercise set has been published publicly yet.

GLACIER tools are used in teaching at the Department of Engineering for
Innovation Medicine of the University of Verona, and material developed for
those sessions will be catalogued here as it is released for public use.

Meanwhile, the closest thing to a set of exercises is
[frost-playground](https://github.com/glacier-project/frost-playground), whose
examples are small enough to modify and extend, and which ships a devcontainer
so a session can start without a local toolchain install.

## What a published lab will look like

A lab entry will carry:

- the topic and the level it assumes;
- how long a session takes;
- what participants need installed, or a container that supplies it;
- a link to the repository holding the exercise material;
- whether solutions are published, and where.

## The physical laboratory

Some of this material relates to the
[ICE laboratory](https://www.icelab.di.univr.it/) in Verona, a working
production line used for research and teaching. Two GLACIER repositories model
it:

- [ice-frost](https://github.com/glacier-project/ice-frost) — the production line
  as Frost components, driven by a recipe-reading scheduler.
- [virtualice-image](https://github.com/glacier-project/virtualice-image) — the
  laboratory's data-collection architecture as a Docker Compose deployment.

Both are public and can be run without access to the laboratory itself.
